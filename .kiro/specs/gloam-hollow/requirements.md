# Gloam Hollow — Requirements

1. The player explores six scenes of a desert ghost town (well square, depot, chapel hill, dry arroyo, main street, lantern tower) in side view.
2. A radial lantern light follows the player's pointer or touch. The player can widen or narrow the light (scroll wheel or pinch); wider light drains oil faster.
3. Oil drains over roughly seven minutes of play; oil tins hidden in scenes refill it.
4. A distant bell tolls every 60 seconds. At the seventh toll (midnight), unreturned wisps scatter again — a soft fail with instant retry.
5. Seven ember-wisps hide across the scenes. The player carries at most two at a time and returns them to the town well.
6. Three original ghost residents (Marigold, the Undersheriff, Little June) offer short, warm dialogue; Little June offers a small fetch beat.
7. Win state: all seven wisps returned before the seventh toll — the tower lantern lights, the town glows scene by scene, and completion time is shown.
8. All art is drawn in code (SVG) and all sound is synthesized with WebAudio. No image files, no audio files, no third-party assets.
