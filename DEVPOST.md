# Gloam Hollow: A Lantern Walk — Devpost submission copy

**Project name:** Gloam Hollow: A Lantern Walk

**Tagline:** Seven lost ember-wisps. One lantern. A desert ghost town that goes dark at the seventh bell.

**What it does:**
Gloam Hollow is a spooky, gentle browser exploration game. On Halloween night you arrive as the new lantern keeper of a desert ghost town whose seven ember-wisps have scattered into the dark. Your lantern throws a living circle of light that follows your hand — widen it to search and burn oil faster, narrow it to make the oil last. Explore six storybook scenes, meet the town's three ghost residents, find the wisps, and carry them back to the old town well before the Hollow Bell tolls midnight.

**How we built it:**
Built with Kiro from spec to ship: the game's design specs and steering documents live in the repo's .kiro folder, and the build was driven from them. The town is drawn entirely in code — layered SVG shapes in a bold coloring-book style — and every sound (wind, the far-off bell, lantern hiss, wisp chimes) is synthesized with WebAudio. No image files, no audio files, no third-party assets. The category is Frankenstein in spirit and in fact: a cozy coloring-book Christmas-town art style, stitched onto a lantern-survival timer and a ghost story — and the patient lived.

**What it runs on:** Any modern browser, desktop or mobile. Static hosting; this repo builds it exactly as submitted.
