# Gloam Hollow: A Lantern Walk

Seven lost ember-wisps. One lantern. A desert ghost town that goes dark at the seventh bell.

Gloam Hollow is a spooky, gentle browser exploration game, built with Kiro for Kiroween 2026. On Halloween night you arrive as the new lantern keeper of a desert ghost town whose seven ember-wisps have scattered into the dark. Your lantern throws a living circle of light that follows your hand — widen it to search and burn oil faster, narrow it to make the oil last. Explore six storybook scenes, meet the town's three ghost residents, find the wisps, and carry them back to the old town well before the Hollow Bell tolls midnight.

**The Frankenstein stitch:** a cozy coloring-book desert-town art style, stitched onto a lantern-survival timer and a ghost story — and the patient lived.

## Built with Kiro

This project is built with Kiro from spec to ship. The design specs and steering documents live in the `.kiro` folder and drive the build. The town is drawn entirely in code — layered SVG shapes in a bold coloring-book style — and every sound (wind, the far-off bell, lantern hiss, wisp chimes) is synthesized with WebAudio. No image files, no audio files, no third-party assets.

## How to play

- **Walk:** move the mouse or touch; the keeper (and the light) follows. Keyboard: ←/→ or A/D.
- **Light:** scroll wheel, pinch, or W/S — wide light searches faster and burns oil faster.
- **Interact:** click or tap wisps, ghosts, oil tins, and anything pale that glimmers.
- **Travel:** walk to a glowing edge, use the on-screen arrows, or press Q/E.
- **Goal:** carry the seven ember-wisps (two at a time) back to the town well in Well Square before the Hollow Bell's seventh toll — and don't let the lantern die.
