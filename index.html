Gloam Hollow

A Lantern Walk

Seven lost ember-wisps. One lantern.
A desert ghost town that goes dark at the seventh bell.

Tonight you are the new lantern keeper of Gloam Hollow. Its seven ember-wisps have scattered into the dark. Walk the town by lamplight, mind your oil, mind the Hollow Bell — and carry every wisp home to the old town well before the seventh toll.

How to keep the Hollow

  • 🔦 Your lantern light follows you. A wide light searches faster but drinks oil; a narrow light sips it.
  • 🪔 Oil lasts about seven minutes. Hidden oil tins refill it. If the lantern dies, the walk is over.
  • 🔔 The Hollow Bell tolls once a minute. At the seventh toll, midnight — unreturned wisps scatter and the night is lost.
  • ✨ Click a wisp your light has found to carry it. You can carry two at a time. Walk them to the town well in Well Square to set them to rest.
  • 👻 The town's three ghosts are friendly. Talk to them — one of them has lost something in the arroyo.
